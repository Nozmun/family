How to Apply

A Straightforward
Referencing Process

We aim to make the referencing process as straightforward as possible. Once you have expressed interest in a property, we will guide you through each step clearly.

  • Initial enquiry and property viewing
  • Application and reference submission
  • Credit check and employment/income verification
  • Previous landlord references (where applicable)
  • Tenancy agreement review and signing
  • Key handover and move-in inventory
